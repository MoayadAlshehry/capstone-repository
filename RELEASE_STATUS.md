It is a version identifier, not evidence of publication or permission activation. ## Version-specific activation

Each public release requires express owner approval of its exact commit/tree, source manifest SHA-256, source ZIP SHA-256 and EVAL-1 wording SHA-256 before publication and activation. Its published receipt records that approval and the covered identity. Source visibility, a branch name, a version identifier or an inactive receipt proposal does not substitute for this record.

The immutable v1.0.0 release and its existing receipt remain scoped to commit `c58e2c9ebfd431dcdc67fe71a18aed7e41006a10`, tree `976fcdbdb57fded4f3f0f9b2b7898ce29a91676f` and the manifest/archive identified in that receipt. They are not rewritten or extended to another release.
